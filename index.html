Mission

One platform, four kinds of people, one shared record of progress.

Every school runs its own portal. Every student follows the same national curriculum. Every parent watches the same report — no matter what device, no matter where the tutor is teaching from.

  • Schools stay independent, data stays connected.Each school gets its own portal and a school admin/teacher, while a super admin keeps the whole network consistent.
  • Industry practitioners teach the practical side.Graphic designers, developers and other tech tutors assign real tasks and grade them directly on the platform.
  • Saturdays are for building, not just watching.Weekly live practicals cover that week's curriculum topic and are recorded for anyone who missed it.
  • Parents see it as it happens.Guardians log in with the details captured at registration to track scores, projects and attendance.
AI GUIDANCE

Two agents, one purpose: point students somewhere useful next.

A holiday-program agent matches every student to training based on location, cost and format after a short end-of-term form. A graduation agent maps completing JSS3 and SS3 students to the tech career and training centre that fit their performance and skills.

Portals

Five roles. One record of truth.

Everyone logs into the same platform — what they see and can act on depends entirely on their role.

⌂

Super Admin

Onboards every school onto EdigiX and holds full add, edit and delete rights across the whole system.

⚙

School Admin/Teacher

Runs one school's portal: registers students, assigns them to tech tutors, appoints other admins, and reviews submitted projects.

✎

Tech Tutor

Industry practitioners, added by the super admin, who teach practicals, grade submitted work, and mark attendance.

▣

Student

Works through the curriculum topic by topic, joins Saturday live sessions, and submits assignments for grading.

♥

Parent / Guardian

Logs in with the details given at registration to follow a child's scores, projects and attendance over time.

AI guidance agents

Matching, not guessing.

Two purpose-built agents turn a student's own answers into a specific next step.

☀

Holiday-program agent

Runs an end-of-term form covering format preference (online or physical), proximity, budget and the skill area a student most needs to strengthen — then returns a shortlist of training centres that fit.

◎

Career-mapping agent

For students completing JSS3 or SS3: weighs overall performance against currently in-demand, profitable tech skills to recommend a career path and a training centre strong in that area.
